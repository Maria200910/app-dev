# My Favorite Movie: Mean Girls (2004)

## Overview
**Mean Girls** is an iconic 2004 teen comedy film directed by Mark Waters and written by **Tina Fey**. It follows **Cady Heron**, a home-schooled teenager who moves to a suburban high school after living in Africa, only to find herself navigating the complex social hierarchy of high school cliques.

---

## The Plastics
* **Regina George**: The ruthless queen bee and leader of The Plastics.
* **Cady Heron**: The new girl who accidentally infiltrates the popular group.
* **Gretchen Wieners**: Regina's rich, insecure sidekick who tries to make *"fetch"* happen.
* **Karen Smith**: The sweet but dim-witted friend with a "sixth sense."

---

## Iconic Rules of The Plastics
1. You can't wear tank tops two days in a row.
2. You can only wear your hair in a ponytail once a week.
3. **On Wednesdays, we wear pink.**

---

## Why I Love It
* **Endless Quoteability**: Packed with legendary lines like *"That's so fetch!"* and *"You can't sit with us!"*
* **Sharp Satire**: A funny yet accurate look at high school popularity and female friendships.
* **Cast Performance**: Outstanding performances by Lindsay Lohan, Rachel McAdams, and the rest of the cast.
